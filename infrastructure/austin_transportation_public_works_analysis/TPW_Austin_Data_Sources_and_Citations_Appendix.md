## RECOMMENDED DATA COLLECTION WORKFLOW

**Step 1 — Budget verification**
Download TPW departmental budget pages from austintexas.gov/budget (FY2024, FY2025 adopted) and extract: total appropriations by fund, FTE count, capital program drawdown schedule.

**Step 2 — Infrastructure condition**
Submit Open Records Request to TPW for: current pavement condition report (PCI scores by street segment), sidewalk gap inventory GIS layer, bridge inspection summary.

**Step 3 — Safety data**
Pull crash data from CRIS for Austin city limits, 2016–2025: filter for fatalities and serious injuries; geocode to census tract; merge with income/race data for equity analysis.

**Step 4 — Flood and demographics**
Download FEMA flood maps for Austin MSA; overlay with ACS income and race data by census tract; calculate population in 100-year floodplain by income quartile.

**Step 5 — Capital project mapping**
Download active CIP project list from austintexas.gov/cip; geocode project locations; overlay with displacement risk maps from Austin Strategic Housing Blueprint.
