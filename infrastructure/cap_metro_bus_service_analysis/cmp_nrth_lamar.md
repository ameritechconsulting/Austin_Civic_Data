North Lamar was a major focus in late 2025, but there is a frustrating "catch" that explains why you are seeing people in wheelchairs getting hit today.
In November 2025, the Austin City Council and the Bond Oversight Commission reviewed the status of the North Lamar Boulevard Corridor Project. While it was "on the list," here is exactly what happened during those meetings and why the sidewalks aren't there yet:
1. The "Reduced Scope" Issue (January – November 2025)
city had to drastically reduce the number of sidewalks planned for North Lamar.
The Reason: Massive drainage problems. Much of North Lamar sits over deep ditches and outdated storm drains.
The Cost: Engineers found that building a simple sidewalk would actually require millions of dollars in "piping and infrastructure" first to prevent flooding.
The Result: Money that was supposed to go toward miles of continuous sidewalks was diverted into underground drainage work. This left many "gaps" (like the one where the accident occurred) still unpaved.
2. The "Streets for Transit" List (November 7, 2025)
On November 7, 2025, the city released the Streets for Transit update. They specifically identified two intersections on North Lamar for safety upgrades:
North Lamar at Morrow Street
North Lamar at Denson DriveIf the accident happened near these spots, they were officially "on the list" for 2026 construction, but the work likely hadn't started yet.
3. The 2026 Bond "Fix
Because of the funding shortfall mentioned in point #1, Council Member Chito Vela (who represents much of North Lamar) used the November 2025 meetings to demand that the upcoming 2026 Mobility Bond (which is being finalized right now) include a massive "re-up" of funds specifically for North Lamar sidewalks that were cut from the previous budget.
