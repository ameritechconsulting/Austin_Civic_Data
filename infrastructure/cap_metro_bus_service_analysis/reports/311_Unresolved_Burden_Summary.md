# 311 Unresolved Ticket Burden Summary

This analysis identifies stops with high 311 call volume where tickets remain unresolved (open/pending-style statuses).

## Most impacted stop in this run

- Stop: 1609 Lavaca/17th (Midblock)
- Total 311 calls: 142
- Unresolved tickets: 113
- Unresolved rate: 79.6%
- Avg open ticket age (days): 507.8
- Composite unresolved burden score: 0.89

## Interpretation note

Use this as an accountability and prioritization signal. It indicates complaint burden and unresolved status concentration, but does not on its own prove intent to ignore complaints.

## Urgent community action: North Lamar sidewalk gap (12300 block)

North Lamar Boulevard remains one of Austin's highest-risk corridors for pedestrians and riders with disabilities where sidewalk gaps persist. Recent improvements, including a new pedestrian beacon installed in February 2026, do not eliminate the immediate access risk at the 12300 block and nearby stops.

### 1) File or confirm a 3-1-1 case for the exact segment
- Channel: Austin 3-1-1 phone line or Austin 3-1-1 mobile app.
- Request type: Sidewalk Repair/New Installation.
- Escalation language to include: life safety risk and wheelchair-access failure.
- Critical detail to document in the case: community-reported wheelchair-user crash/incident at or near the location.

Why this matters: sidewalk prioritization is case-driven. If the exact segment is not documented, it can remain low-priority.

### 2) Escalate to the North Lamar Corridor program team
- Program: North Lamar Boulevard Mobility Project.
- Contact: corridors@austintexas.gov.
- Ask: move the specific block and stop-area sidewalk gap into an accelerated safety timeline.

### 3) Request district-level emergency mobility intervention
- Likely districts for northern North Lamar: District 4 (Jose "Chito" Vela) and District 7.
- Ask offices to request an Emergency Mobility Improvement for the affected stop area.
- Interim option to request while awaiting full reconstruction: temporary asphalt sidewalk connection and/or accessible landing pad.

### 4) File formal ADA accessibility complaints (City and transit agency)
- City of Austin Office of Civil Rights: 512-974-3451 or officeofcivilrights@austintexas.gov.
- CapMetro ADA Coordinator (Martin Kareithi): 512-389-7583 or ccr-accessibility@capmetro.org.

Purpose: formal complaints trigger documented compliance review and investigation pathways for inaccessible transit access routes.
