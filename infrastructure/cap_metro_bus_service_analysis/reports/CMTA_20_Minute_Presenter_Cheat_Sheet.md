# 20-Minute Presenter Cheat Sheet

Use this when you need to present quickly without reading full notes.

## Opening (0:00-2:00)
- We built a public-data baseline on transit stop adequacy and accountability.
- Focus: where high-service stops still face unresolved infrastructure and safety burden.

## Most Pressing Concerns (2:00-12:00)
1. Amenity deficits at high-priority stops
- 80% missing shelter
- 64% missing benches

2. High-risk stop concentration
- Top examples: 1609 Lavaca/17th, Guadalupe/16th, Lavaca/4th
- Use district and tract concentration charts to localize where burden clusters.

3. Safety and ADA signals
- Safety complaints share: 10.65%
- ADA-related records in baseline window: 342

4. Complaint burden remains high
- Full-year totals are still elevated across 2023-2025
- 2026 is partial-year and not directly comparable

5. Responsibility coordination
- CapMetro share: 82.62%
- City share: 12.40%
- Need explicit joint resolution workflow

6. Unresolved complaint concentration
- 1609 Lavaca/17th (Midblock): 142 calls, 113 unresolved (79.6%)
- Guadalupe/16th Street: 138 calls, 116 unresolved (84.1%)
- Lavaca/4th: 135 calls, 106 unresolved (78.5%)
- Use unresolved burden as a closure-accountability trigger
- Add tract unresolved chart when audience asks for neighborhood-level accountability.

7. Tract density and digital divide (new)
- 1,250,625 records mapped to 290 tracts (Oct 2021-Apr 2026)
- All 25 high-demand stops fall in very high or extreme-density complaint tracts
- District 4: highest gap index and highest phone dependency (65% phone)
- Citywide channel split: 62.9% phone, 35.8% digital
- Key line: this is not a demand-visibility problem; it is a supply and response problem

## Action Plan (12:00-18:00)
- Prioritize top 10 risk stops for shelter/bench fixes
- Joint CMTA and City tracker for high-call unresolved complaints
- Monthly dashboard and closure targets for recurring stops

## North Lamar emergency script (use when asked about 12300 block)
- Acknowledge the life-safety urgency: persistent sidewalk gaps and disability-access risk remain despite recent signal/beacon upgrades.
- Direct immediate 3-1-1 filing for the exact segment: request type "Sidewalk Repair/New Installation," tagged as life safety.
- Tell residents to escalate to corridors@austintexas.gov (North Lamar Boulevard Mobility Project) and request timeline acceleration.
- Ask District 4 or District 7 offices to pursue an Emergency Mobility Improvement with interim accessible connection treatment.
- Provide formal ADA channels:
	- City Office of Civil Rights: 512-974-3451, officeofcivilrights@austintexas.gov
	- CapMetro ADA Coordinator: 512-389-7583, ccr-accessibility@capmetro.org

## Close and Ask (18:00-20:00)
- Approve focused corrective plan
- Approve monthly evidence refresh
- Approve unresolved-ticket closure targets and monthly reporting by stop
- Approve standing district/tract monitoring with digital-access split
