# CMTA Baseline
## 5-Minute Executive Brief

Audience: City executives, board chairs, leadership staff  
Duration: 5 minutes

## 1) Core Finding (1:00)
This baseline shows that many high-service transit stops still lack basic rider protections, and complaint burden remains concentrated at specific locations.

## 2) Three Numbers to Remember (1:30)
- 80% of high-priority stops are missing shelter.
- 64% of high-priority stops are missing benches.
- 342 ADA-related records appear in the baseline window.

## 3) Unresolved 311 Burden (1:00)
- 1609 Lavaca/17th (Midblock): 142 calls, 113 unresolved (79.6%)
- Guadalupe/16th Street: 138 calls, 116 unresolved (84.1%)
- Lavaca/4th: 135 calls, 106 unresolved (78.5%)
- These high-volume unresolved patterns should trigger closure-time accountability.

## 4) Risk Pattern (1:00)
Highest-risk stops repeatedly show a combined pattern of high service activity, high complaints, and persistent unresolved conditions.

Examples:
- 1609 Lavaca/17th (Midblock)
- Guadalupe/16th Street
- Lavaca/4th

## 5) Accountability Pattern (0:45)
Issue attribution is primarily CapMetro-coded (82.62%), with a material City-attributed share (12.40%), requiring explicit coordination for fast resolution.

## 6) Decision Ask (0:45)
Approve three actions:
1. Top-10 stop corrective package for shelter and benches
2. Joint CMTA-City tracker for unresolved high-call complaints
3. Monthly evidence refresh and public progress reporting

## 7) North Lamar Emergency Addendum (0:30)
- Location focus: North Lamar 12300 block sidewalk gap near transit access.
- Immediate action path: 3-1-1 filing (Sidewalk Repair/New Installation) with life-safety and wheelchair-access language.
- Escalation contact: North Lamar Boulevard Mobility Project at corridors@austintexas.gov.
- District action ask: District 4 or 7 emergency mobility intervention with interim accessible connection treatment.
- ADA compliance channels:
	- City Office of Civil Rights: 512-974-3451, officeofcivilrights@austintexas.gov
	- CapMetro ADA Coordinator: 512-389-7583, ccr-accessibility@capmetro.org

## One-line close
This baseline provides enough evidence to act now while building phase-2 spatial equity detail.
