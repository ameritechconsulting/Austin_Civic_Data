Executive Summary
Program at a Glance
The Neighborhood Services Unit delivers the OASIS Community Action Program under the Community Services Block Grant (CSBG), administered by the City of Austin Public Health Department and overseen at the state level by the Texas Health and Human Services Commission (HHSC). Operating through 7 neighborhood centers, NSU provides a continuum of services targeting Austin's most economically vulnerable residents — from emergency crisis intervention through sustainable career and education pathways.

Headline Strengths

  • All 5 FNPI success rates improved 7–10 percentage points over 5 years
  • TOP success rate of 91.4% over 5 years — exceptionally strong
  • 34% enrollment growth while improving per-client cost efficiency
  • Emergency service reliance declining as upstream programming scales
  • FY2026 budget on pace — 46.7% expended at Q2 midpoint

Priority Gaps

  • FNPI 5b (School Readiness) lags all other indicators at 68.1%
  • Employment training-to-placement conversion static at ~42%
  • Northeast Center TOP outcomes 36% below center average
  • CSBG federal reauthorization creates annual funding uncertainty
  • Annual reporting framework undercounts multi-year pathway completions

Key Observations

  • FNPI 4e (Employment Obtained) showed the greatest improvement at +10.5 pts, reflecting expanded employer partnerships
  • All five indicators exceeded the national CSBG benchmark of 68% by FY2025
  • FNPI 5 (Credential Attainment) strongest positive trajectory — post-secondary vocational programs account for ~62% of FY2025 achievements
  • 97.5% capture rate in FY2025 (enrolled vs. target) reflects improved intake screening

Areas for Investment

  • FNPI 5b (School Readiness) remains lowest performer at 71.2% — COVID disruptions to early childhood programming still echoing
  • FNPI 5d (GED/Diploma) at 70.5% — longest average completion time (18 months) complicates annual measurement
  • Gap between FNPI 4 (skill-building) and 4e (placement) has narrowed but persists at 0.7 pts — employer partnership expansion needed

Service Mix Shift — A Positive Indicator

  • Emergency services (SRV 7a) declined 23.2% — indicating the program is engaging clients earlier and reducing crisis-level need
  • Case management (SRV 3a.1) is the largest and fastest-growing service (+51.1%) — confirming NSU's whole-family approach is scaling
  • Employment training to placement conversion (SRV 4c → 4i) has improved but remains at ~42% — the primary systemic gap to address
  • Housing & Rent: 4,855 evictions prevented; direct rent payment assistance provided to 3,210 households across 6,935 total housing contacts over 5 years
  • Food & Nutrition: 50,740 units delivered across food pantry access, SNAP enrollment navigation, and direct emergency food aid — 14,830 unique households served over 5 years
  • Health referrals to clinical care: 2,938 over 5 years from health navigation services

Depth of Poverty Reduction

  • Clients are entering deeper in poverty year-over-year (avg starting FPL declining from 62.4% to 58.9%)
  • Despite deeper starting points, FPL gains are accelerating — +44.9 pts in FY2025 vs. +31.7 pts in FY2021
  • FY2025: 301 families crossed from below 100% FPL to 103.8% FPL average — economic self-sufficiency achieved
  • Multi-service coordination (employment + housing + health) is the driver of deepest poverty exits

Strategic Findings & Recommendations
FY2026–FY2028 Priority Actions
Six prioritized recommendations based on five-year performance data, center-level analysis, and financial trajectory. Estimated investment: $628,000 over the planning period.
Priority 1 — Employment
Launch Employer Partnership Cohort
Structured employer engagement program at Dove Springs, Rundberg, and Rosewood-Zaragosa — the three highest-enrollment employment centers. Target major Austin employers in healthcare, construction, and hospitality to close the 42% training-to-placement conversion gap.
$85,000 FY2026 Q3
Priority 2 — Early Childhood
Hire 2 Early Childhood Specialists
Place dedicated early childhood specialists at George Morales and Rosewood-Zaragosa — the centers with the highest child care service volumes. Projected to increase FNPI 5b school readiness rates by 4–6 percentage points annually.
$142,000/yr FY2026 Q4
Priority 3 — Data Systems
Multi-Year Outcome Tracking Dashboard
Implement longitudinal cohort tracking for clients on 18–24 month education or career pathways (most common in FNPI 5d). Current annual reporting undercounts these successes. One-time technology investment to correct systemic measurement gap.
$32,000 FY2026 Q3–Q4
Priority 4 — Center Equity
Northeast Center Capacity Audit
Northeast Center's TOP achievement (34) is 36% below center average. Commission a case management audit and staffing review to identify root causes — whether staffing, population composition, or service mix requires adjustment for the senior/veteran demographic.
$18,000 FY2026 Q3
Priority 5 — Financial Risk
Establish CSBG Contingency Reserve
Given CSBG federal reauthorization uncertainty, work with the City Budget Office to establish a contingency reserve at 15% of annual contract value (~$351,000). Protects service continuity from mid-year federal funding disruptions.
$351,000 FY2027
Priority 6 — Efficiency
Cost-per-TOP Reduction Target
Set a formal target to reduce cost per TOP outcome by 5% annually through FY2028. Current cost: $5,882 per TOP outcome. Achieved through enrollment growth and service coordination improvements — no additional budget required.
Staff time only FY2027 onward

Appendix
Glossary of Key Terms
Reference definitions for CSBG reporting framework terminology used throughout this analysis.
CSBG — Community Services Block Grant: federal anti-poverty grant program
NSU — Neighborhood Services Unit: City of Austin program operator
OASIS — Community Action Program brand name for Austin NSU services
FNPI — Family/Individual National Performance Indicator: outcome measure
SRV — Service indicator: unit of service delivery measure
TOP — Transition Out of Poverty: composite family self-sufficiency outcome
ROMA — Results Oriented Management and Accountability: CSBG framework
FPL — Federal Poverty Level
NPI — National Performance Indicator
HHSC — Texas Health and Human Services Commission
COATES Act — Community Opportunities, Accountability, Training and Education Services Act (1998)
ACC — Austin Community College: key NSU employer/education partner
