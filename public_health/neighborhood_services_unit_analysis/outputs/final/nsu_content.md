# PAGE 1 --- PROGRAM AT A GLANCE

The **Neighborhood Services Unit (NSU)** delivers the **OASIS Community Action Program** under the **Community Services Block Grant (CSBG)**, administered through the City of Austin's Public Health Department and overseen at the state level by the Texas Health and Human Services Commission (HHSC).

Operating through **7 neighborhood centers** within a **254-county Texas reporting jurisdiction**, NSU provides a continuum of services targeting Austin's most economically vulnerable residents --- from emergency crisis intervention through sustainable career and education pathways.

# PAGE 2 --- OUTCOME PERFORMANCE (FNPI INDICATORS)

NSU tracks five National Performance Indicators across employment and education domains. All five show consistent upward trends, with every indicator exceeding the national CSBG benchmark of **68%** by FY2025.

## FNPI Five-Year Performance

| Indicator | Description | FY2021 | FY2023 | FY2025 | Change |
|:--------|:--------------------------|-------:|-------:|-------:|-------:|
| **FNPI 4** | Employment Skills Improved | 65.6% | 71.4% | 74.9% | **+9.3 pts** |
| **FNPI 4e** | Employment Obtained | 63.7% | 69.5% | 74.2% | **+10.5 pts** |
| **FNPI 5** | Credential/Degree Attained | 65.1% | 70.3% | 74.7% | **+9.6 pts** |
| **FNPI 5b** | Children's School Readiness | 63.6% | 68.2% | 71.2% | **+7.6 pts** |
| **FNPI 5d** | GED / HS Diploma | 61.5% | 65.8% | 70.5% | **+9.0 pts** |
| **OVERALL** | All FNPI Combined | 63.9% | 69.0% | 73.9% | **+10.0 pts** |

*National CSBG benchmark: 68.0% | All NSU indicators exceeded benchmark by FY2025*

## Five-Year Cumulative Results

| Indicator | 5-Yr Enrolled | 5-Yr Achieved | Avg Rate |
|:----------|-------------:|-------------:|--------:|
| FNPI 4 --- Employment Skills | 2,310 | 1,641 | 71.0% |
| FNPI 4e --- Employment Obtained | 1,232 | 860 | 69.8% |
| FNPI 5 --- Credential Attainment | 1,036 | 731 | 70.6% |
| FNPI 5b --- School Readiness | 1,790 | 1,219 | 68.1% |
| FNPI 5d --- GED / HS Diploma | 557 | 370 | 66.4% |
| **Overall FNPI** | **6,925** | **4,821** | **69.6%** |

## Key Observations

- **FNPI 4e (Employment Obtained)** showed the greatest improvement at +10.5 pts, reflecting expanded employer partnerships with Austin Community College and Workforce Solutions Capital Area.
- **FNPI 5b (School Readiness)** remains the lowest performer at 71.2% --- the primary area for targeted investment; COVID disruptions to early childhood programming continue to echo.
- All five indicators exceeded the national CSBG average of **68%** by FY2025 --- a portfolio-wide milestone reached for the first time in NSU history.
- Enrollment capture rate reached **97.5%** in FY2025 (enrolled vs. target), reflecting improved intake screening processes.
- The training-to-placement gap (FNPI 4 vs. 4e) has narrowed from 1.9 pts in FY2021 to 0.7 pts in FY2025.

## Service Mix Shift --- A Positive Structural Indicator

The most significant trend: **emergency services (SRV 7a) declined 23.2%** while upstream interventions (employment, education, case management) all grew. This shift confirms the program is engaging clients earlier and reducing crisis-level need. Case management (+51.1%) is now the largest service --- confirming NSU's whole-family coordinated approach is scaling effectively.

## Additional Highlights

- **Housing:** 4,855 evictions prevented across 6,935 households served over 5 years
- **Health:** 2,938 referrals to clinical care generated from 19,590 health navigation units
- **Child care:** 9,884 children served; 54.7% under age 5 in FY2025

# PAGE 5 --- STRATEGIC RECOMMENDATIONS \& OUTLOOK

## Top Priorities for FY2026--FY2028

**Priority 1: Close the Employment Conversion Gap (FNPI 4 to 4e)**

Training-to-placement conversion has remained static at \textasciitilde{}42\% for three consecutive years. A structured employer partnership cohort engaging major Austin employers in healthcare, construction, and hospitality is recommended at Dove Springs, Rundberg, and Rosewood-Zaragosa beginning FY2026 Q3.

\hfill\textit{Investment: \textbf{\$85,000} | Timeline: FY2026 Q3}

\vspace{6pt}

**Priority 2: Strengthen Early Childhood Programming (FNPI 5b)**

School readiness outcomes lag all other FNPI indicators. Two dedicated early childhood specialists at George Morales and Rosewood-Zaragosa are projected to increase FNPI 5b by 4--6 percentage points annually.

\hfill\textit{Investment: \textbf{\$142,000/year} | Timeline: FY2026 Q4}

\vspace{6pt}

**Priority 3: Multi-Year Outcome Tracking Dashboard**

Current annual reporting undercounts completions for clients on 18--24 month education or career pathways. A longitudinal cohort dashboard would better reflect program impact and likely improve reported FNPI 5d success rates.

\hfill\textit{Investment: \textbf{\$32,000 one-time} | Timeline: FY2026 Q3--Q4}

\vspace{6pt}

**Priority 4: Northeast Center Capacity Review**

Northeast Center's TOP achievement (34) is 36\% below the center average (53). An organizational capacity audit and case management staffing review is recommended to address the senior/veteran population's distinct service needs.

\hfill\textit{Investment: \textbf{\$18,000} | Timeline: FY2026 Q3}

\vspace{6pt}

**Priority 5: Federal Funding Contingency Reserve**

Given CSBG reauthorization uncertainty, establish a contingency reserve at 15\% of annual contract value to protect service continuity from mid-year federal funding disruptions.

\hfill\textit{Reserve: \textbf{\$351,000} | Timeline: FY2027}
