What is Austin Nexus?

Austin Nexus is the civic intelligence platform of Ameritech Consulting Group — providing public-sector operational analytics, GIS mapping, and reporting solutions that transform fragmented public data into actionable operational insights.

We specialize in integrating infrastructure, transportation, procurement, GIS, workforce, and operational datasets into clear analytical dashboards, visualizations, and reporting systems that support transparency, operational visibility, and decision-making.

Every dataset, methodology, and finding is published openly on GitHub so residents, journalists, advocates, and policymakers can verify, replicate, and build on the work.

  • 🔗

    Systems-Level Approach

    We integrate cross-agency datasets — GIS, infrastructure, procurement, workforce — into unified, actionable operational insights.

  • 📋

    Source-Cited Analysis

    Every claim links back to a primary government source — City data portals, U.S. Census, CapMetro GTFS feeds, and open records.

  • 🏙️

    District-Level Equity Focus

    All analyses are disaggregated by Austin's 10 council districts to surface geographic equity gaps and inform targeted policy.
