Key Findings & Equity Observations
Summary of patterns identified across contract data, vendor certification, and workforce analysis.

Contract Concentration

  • Top 5 vendors hold 6% of all professional services contract value — a high concentration risk.
  • Top 10 vendors (9%) are almost entirely large national engineering firms, not local Austin businesses.
  • AECOM alone holds $1.0M across 1 contracts — the City's single largest professional services vendor.
  • Austin Water Utility accounts for the majority of professional services spend, driven by capital infrastructure projects.

Small & Minority Business Gaps

  • Only 128 vendors hold SBE certification — a small pool relative to total vendor market.
  • SBE firms are largely absent from top-dollar engineering and technical contracts.
  • MBE/WBE participation data is limited in open data — a transparency gap.
  • The Civilitude, LLC (SBE) appears in both the certified vendor list and the top contracts — a rare crossover.

Workforce Equity

  • City workforce demographics show disproportionate representation gaps in technical and leadership roles.
  • Apprenticeship and internship positions offer a pathway — but distribution by ethnicity warrants monitoring.
  • Austin Energy and Austin Water are the largest employers of apprentices but have uneven demographic profiles.

Recommended Next Steps

  • Cross-reference repeat vendors with City Council agenda approvals and board award dates.
  • File open records request for MBE/WBE utilization reports by department.
  • Map SBE vendor locations against council districts to assess geographic equity.
  • Monitor contract renewal patterns — which vendors are renewed vs. competitively bid.
